# Positioning relative to the OSDR AI/ML AWG retina project

RetinaCellTwin should be presented as an independent, open and reproducibility-first implementation that can complement the existing AWG Digital Twin Project led by Jian Gong.

It should not claim ownership of the AWG project, reuse unpublished group material without permission, or imply that the AWG endorsed its conclusions.

## Potential contribution to the group

- A fully versioned open-data ingestion and provenance layer.
- A transparent baseline for RR-9 transcriptomic and imaging endpoints.
- An independent transferability audit spanning OSD-255, OSD-758 and OSD-203.
- A falsification result showing that the RR-9 signature is not a stable universal gravity signature.
- A bounded seven-day radiation-compatible hypothesis selected by a random-panel benchmark.
- Explicit separation of observed, derived and synthetic layers.
- A testable interface contract for future synthetic-data models.
- A bridge from the PlantCellTwin reproducibility pattern to mammalian retinal biology.

## Sensible first collaboration request

Share the repository after the v0.2 workflow and frozen outputs are archived. Ask whether the cross-mission audit and its radiation-compatible candidate set could support one of the group's unmet validation needs, and invite technical critique before proposing shared authorship or integration.
