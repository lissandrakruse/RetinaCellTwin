# Supplementary computational methods

## S1. Evidence policy

Observed values are restricted to public NASA OSDR files. Derived statistics are generated by versioned scripts. Interface probabilities, utilities, costs and portfolio solutions are hypothetical user inputs and are excluded from biological claims.

## S2. Dataset roles

- OSD-255: RR-9 right-retina bulk RNA-seq discovery.
- OSD-557 and OSD-568: left-eye transformed microscopy measurements used only as study-level context.
- OSD-758: independent retinal RNA-seq artificial-gravity transfer audit.
- OSD-203: longitudinal gamma-radiation and hindlimb-unloading analogue audit.

Accessions, filenames and retrieval records are frozen in `data/datasets.json` and `data/provenance.json`.

## S3. RR-9 discovery panel

The panel contains every OSD-255 gene with NASA-reported Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted P < 0.05 for Space Flight versus Ground Control. Version suffixes are removed from Ensembl identifiers before cross-dataset mapping. No OSD-758 or OSD-203 result is used to select the 362 panel genes.

## S4. Cross-mission transfer and artificial-gravity audit

OSD-255 flight-versus-ground effects are compared with OSD-758 microgravity-versus-ground effects by Spearman correlation and exact one-sided direction testing. Apparent attenuation at each artificial-gravity level is defined from absolute effect-size reduction relative to microgravity and compared with the non-panel transcriptome background by Mann–Whitney tests. A strict candidate additionally requires a direct OSD-758 microgravity-versus-in-flight-1G association passing the frozen threshold.

## S5. Ground-analogue triangulation

OSD-758 microgravity-versus-in-flight-1G effects are compared with OSD-203 hindlimb-unloading contrasts. OSD-758 in-flight-1G-versus-ground effects are compared with OSD-203 radiation contrasts at seven days, one month and four months. For each prespecified comparison, the observed panel correlation is benchmarked against 10,000 equal-sized random panels drawn from the same common-gene universe with a fixed seed. Benjamini-Hochberg correction is applied across the time-by-mechanism family.

## S6. Processed-value sensitivity audit

For ten contrasts, group mean differences, Welch tests and Benjamini-Hochberg correction are recomputed from public OSDR variance-stabilized matrices using sample-group metadata. Spearman correlation and directional agreement quantify consistency with NASA log2 fold changes. Genes with exactly zero variance in both compared groups are counted in each machine-readable contrast summary; undefined standardized effects or tests do not contribute finite FDR values. This starts after NASA normalization and is not a FASTQ-level reproduction.

## S7. Count-level effect audit

Public STAR unnormalized gene-count matrices are normalized independently with DESeq-style median-of-ratios size factors using genes positive in every sample. Genes with mean normalized count below 10 within a contrast are removed. Unshrunken log2 ratios of group means use a 0.5 pseudocount. The resulting effects are compared with NASA DESeq2 log2 fold changes; inferential p-values are not reconstructed.

## S8. Gene Ontology analysis

MGI Biological Process annotations are read from GAF 2.2, excluding `NOT` qualifiers, and propagated through `is_a` and `part_of` ancestors in the GO basic ontology. One-sided Fisher tests evaluate three prespecified foreground/background pairs, with Benjamini-Hochberg correction within each analysis. Source sizes and SHA-256 digests are frozen in `data/functional_sources.json`.

## S9. Retinal cell-class context

The 12 canonical major-class anchors shown in Figure 1E of Li et al. (2024; doi:10.1016/j.isci.2024.109916) are frozen in `data/retinal_cell_anchors.tsv`. NASA log2 fold changes and FDR values are reported for four bulk contrasts. The audit neither fits a mixture model nor estimates cell proportions and cannot separate altered cellular composition from regulation within a cell class.

## S10. Imaging comparisons

Seven endpoints are declared before testing. Space Flight and Ground Control groups are compared with Welch and Mann–Whitney tests, with FDR correction across endpoints. Hedges' g is reported as an effect-size summary. Transcriptomic and imaging accessions use opposite eyes and are not treated as paired.

## S11. Unpaired cross-modal evidence matrix

Four project-scope domains are assembled after the modality-specific analyses. Existing imaging FDR values, atlas-anchor results and GO results are reported side by side. No new joint statistic is calculated. “Study-level contextual convergence” requires an imaging endpoint passing the existing Welch FDR boundary plus independently supported transcriptomic context in the same broad domain; it does not imply sample pairing, mediation or causality.

## S12. Software and verification

Python dependencies are exactly pinned in `requirements-lock.txt`; `environment.yml` and `requirements.txt` provide bounded convenience specifications. `Dockerfile`, `run_all.sh`, the continuous-integration workflow and `run_verification.sh` define the executable release path. All figures are deterministic SVGs produced from frozen result files.
