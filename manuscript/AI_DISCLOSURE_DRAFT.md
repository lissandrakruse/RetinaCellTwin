# Generative-AI use disclosure — draft and audit checklist

## Proposed end-of-paper statement

During development of this manuscript and its software package, OpenAI ChatGPT with Codex was used to assist with literature discovery, code and documentation drafting, organization of the manuscript, and English-language editing. It was not treated as an author or contributor and did not supply empirical observations. Numerical results reported in the manuscript were generated from the cited public datasets by the versioned scripts and verification tests in the repository. Before submission, the named human authors must review the complete manuscript and code, verify all citations and numerical claims, approve the disclosed uses, and accept full responsibility for the accuracy, originality and integrity of the work. A persistent record describing the relevant prompts, outputs, dates and human verification will be provided at **[insert supplementary-file or repository DOI and file path]**.

## Facts presently supported by the project record

- Generative AI assisted with source discovery, code drafting, documentation and language editing.
- Public NASA OSDR files, rather than model-generated values, are the source of observed data.
- The repository contains deterministic scripts and tests that can regenerate and audit reported results.
- AI is not named as an author or CRediT contributor.

## Items that must be completed by humans before submission

- Record exact product and model names/versions where available, the period of use and the purposes for each material interaction.
- Export the relevant prompts and outputs or create a faithful, reviewable supplement that meets the editor's interpretation of the policy.
- Remove private credentials, personal data and irrelevant conversation before public deposition, without concealing material research instructions or outputs.
- Have the authors inspect every cited source directly and record corrections.
- Have the authors review every executable analysis and confirm that the code implements the described method.
- Insert the persistent location of the AI-use supplement in both this statement and Data Availability.
- Obtain approval of the exact disclosure from every author.

## Policy basis

The current [GigaScience editorial policy](https://academic.oup.com/gigascience/pages/editorial_policies_and_reporting_standards) permits generative AI writing tools when use is transparent and does not compromise empirical data or conclusions. It prohibits AI authorship, assigns full responsibility to the human authors, asks for an end-of-paper summary and requests that relevant outputs be included as supplementary material in GigaDB or another open repository.
